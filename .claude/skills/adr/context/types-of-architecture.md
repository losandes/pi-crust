# Types of Engineering Architecture

## Conceptual Architecture & Design

Conceptual Architecture & Design is used to describe strategic investment initiatives with clear mission and value propositions. The output of conceptual architecture & design might include inspirational materials, competitive analysis, choices that define what we're going to do and not going to do, and/or *relationships* between people, technology, business, and information.

In building architecture, the artist’s rendering of buildings is an example of conceptual architecture & design.  This is similar.

### How is Conceptual Architecture used?

- Clarify the future vision and seek a shared understanding of the priorities, objectives, and challenges
- Generate organizational excitement for the ideas generated during the workshop
- Seek initial feedback from early adopters, consumers, and effected parties

## Logical Architecture & Design

Logical Architecture & Design is used to promote a deeper comprehension of, and alignment with the conceptual architecture & design. In logical architecture & design, we produce context for multiple audiences to express the *flow* between people, technology, business, and information. We model and compare options that enable the choices that were defined in conceptual architecture. We might make decisions about those options.

In building architecture, CAD layouts, and traffic flow diagrams are examples of logical architecture & design.

### How is Logical Architecture used?

- Inform, enable, and make strategic decisions, while deferring decisions that can or should be made during implementation phases
- Identify areas of complexity and risk
- Clarify effort that depends on other effort, departments, or vendors
- Design what the implementation / build team needs to be
- Envision the customer/user experience

## Physical Architecture & Design

Physical Architecture & Design is used to support compliance, maintenance, and planning initiatives. In physical architecture & design, we define the current, or desired state of systems that have been, or need to be implemented.

In building architecture, Blueprints are an example of physical architecture & design.

### How is Physical Architecture used?

- Communicate project design & planning to stakeholders
- Communicate design & requirements to implementors and tactical operations
- Onboarding talent on to the project
- Inform decisions and troubleshooting for support & maintenance
